2026-11-02 | Dinner | Creamy Tuscan Chicken with Cauliflower Mash | SERVES 4. You need: 1 1/2 lb chicken breast, sliced; 2 cups baby spinach; 1/2 cup sun-dried tomatoes in oil, drained and chopped; 3 cloves garlic, minced; 1 cup heavy cream; 1/2 cup grated Parmesan; 1 head cauliflower or 1 bag frozen florets; 2 tbsp butter; 2 tbsp cream cheese. 1. Steam cauliflower 10-12 minutes, mash with butter and cream cheese, salt to taste. 2. Season chicken, sear in a hot oiled skillet 4-5 minutes per side, remove. 3. In the same pan, cook garlic 1 minute, add cream, Parmesan, tomatoes and spinach, simmer 3-4 minutes until thick. 4. Return chicken, spoon sauce over, simmer 2 minutes. Cut chicken into strips for the 3-year-old. Serve over mash.
