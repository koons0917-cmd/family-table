# Month-Ahead Meal Plan — November 2026

Same template as the last few weeks: Monday to Saturday each week (Sunday off), lunch, snack and dinner only — no breakfast. Low-carb, kid-friendly, for 2 adults + 2 kids (ages 3 and 6).

**Budget: $200 per week is a shopping cap to shop against, NOT a promised HEB shelf price.** Each week's grocery estimate below is a planning estimate under that cap, not a shelf-price promise. Prices can move at pickup, and substitutions can change the total.

All 24 dinners are new and different from each other and from her recent dinners. None of these are reused: mini cauliflower-crust pizzas, spaghetti squash spaghetti bake, mini turkey meatloaf muffins, beef & broccoli over cauliflower rice, cheeseburger lettuce cups, sheet-pan chicken fajitas in lettuce wraps, or Philly cheesesteak stuffed peppers.

### Monday, November 2, 2026 — 2026-11-02

- **Lunch:** Ham & cheese roll-ups + cucumber rounds
- **Snack:** Strawberries + cheese stick
- **Dinner:** Creamy Tuscan Chicken with Cauliflower Mash

  **Dinner recipe:** SERVES 4. You need: 1 1/2 lb chicken breast, sliced; 2 cups baby spinach; 1/2 cup sun-dried tomatoes in oil, drained and chopped; 3 cloves garlic, minced; 1 cup heavy cream; 1/2 cup grated Parmesan; 1 head cauliflower or 1 bag frozen florets; 2 tbsp butter; 2 tbsp cream cheese. 1. Steam cauliflower 10-12 minutes, mash with butter and cream cheese, salt to taste. 2. Season chicken, sear in a hot oiled skillet 4-5 minutes per side, remove. 3. In the same pan, cook garlic 1 minute, add cream, Parmesan, tomatoes and spinach, simmer 3-4 minutes until thick. 4. Return chicken, spoon sauce over, simmer 2 minutes. Cut chicken into strips for the 3-year-old. Serve over mash.

### Tuesday, November 3, 2026 — 2026-11-03

- **Lunch:** Chicken salad lettuce cups
- **Snack:** Celery boats with cream cheese
- **Dinner:** Turkey Taco Lettuce Boats

  **Dinner recipe:** SERVES 4. You need: 1 1/2 lb ground turkey; 1 tbsp taco seasoning (no sugar added); 8-12 butter lettuce or romaine leaves; 1 cup shredded cheddar; 1 cup no-sugar-added salsa; 1/2 cup sour cream; 1 tomato, diced. 1. Brown turkey in a skillet 6-8 minutes, breaking it small; drain fat, stir in taco seasoning and 2 tbsp water, simmer 2 minutes. 2. Set out lettuce leaves, turkey, cheese, salsa, sour cream and tomato family-style. 3. Everyone builds their own boats. For the 3-year-old, use a soft inner leaf and a small spoonful of turkey.

### Wednesday, November 4, 2026 — 2026-11-04

- **Lunch:** Turkey & cheese kabob boxes
- **Snack:** Cheese cubes + cucumber with pickle dip
- **Dinner:** Lemon Butter Baked Salmon with Roasted Broccoli

  **Dinner recipe:** SERVES 4. You need: 1 1/2 lb salmon fillets, cut into 4 pieces; 3 tbsp butter, melted; 1 lemon, half juiced, half sliced; 2 cloves garlic, minced; 4 cups broccoli florets; 1 tbsp olive oil. 1. Heat oven to 400F. Toss broccoli with oil and salt on a sheet pan. 2. Place salmon on the pan, brush with butter, garlic and lemon juice, top with lemon slices. 3. Bake 12-15 minutes until salmon flakes and broccoli is tender at the edges. 4. Cut salmon into small flakes for the kids. Serve with any pan juices spooned over.

### Thursday, November 5, 2026 — 2026-11-05

- **Lunch:** Egg salad + veggie sticks
- **Snack:** Hard-boiled egg halves + cucumber
- **Dinner:** Chicken Alfredo over Zucchini Noodles

  **Dinner recipe:** SERVES 4. You need: 1 1/2 lb chicken breast, sliced into strips; 4 cups zucchini noodles; 2 tbsp butter; 2 cloves garlic, minced; 1 cup heavy cream; 3/4 cup grated Parmesan; salt and pepper. 1. Season chicken, cook in buttered skillet 5-6 minutes until cooked through, remove. 2. In the same pan, cook garlic 1 minute, add cream, simmer 3 minutes, stir in Parmesan until smooth. 3. Warm zucchini noodles in the sauce 2 minutes only, so they stay firm. 4. Return chicken, toss, serve at once. Cut noodles short for the 3-year-old.

### Friday, November 6, 2026 — 2026-11-06

- **Lunch:** Tuna melts on bell-pepper boats
- **Snack:** Pepperoni chips + guacamole cup
- **Dinner:** Garlic Butter Steak Bites with Green Beans

  **Dinner recipe:** SERVES 4. You need: 1 1/2 lb sirloin, cut into bite-size cubes; 3 tbsp butter; 3 cloves garlic, minced; 1 lb green beans, trimmed; 1 tbsp olive oil. 1. Toss green beans with oil and salt, roast at 425F for 12-15 minutes until just tender. 2. Sear steak cubes in a very hot skillet 2-3 minutes, in batches, until browned. 3. Return all steak to the pan, add butter and garlic, toss 1 minute until glossy. 4. Serve steak bites with green beans. Cut steak small for the kids.

### Saturday, November 7, 2026 — 2026-11-07

- **Lunch:** Turkey & cream cheese pinwheels + cucumber rounds
- **Snack:** Thin apple slices + cheese cubes
- **Dinner:** Almond-Crusted Chicken Tenders with Cauliflower Mac and Cheese

  **Dinner recipe:** SERVES 4. You need: 1 1/2 lb chicken tenders; 1 cup almond flour; 1/2 cup grated Parmesan; 1 egg, beaten; 1 head cauliflower, cut into florets; 1 cup shredded cheddar; 2 tbsp cream cheese; 2 tbsp butter. 1. Heat oven to 425F. Mix almond flour and Parmesan. Dip tenders in egg, then coating, place on a lined pan. 2. Bake 15-18 minutes until golden and cooked through. 3. Meanwhile steam cauliflower 10 minutes, stir with butter, cream cheese and cheddar until melted and creamy. 4. Serve tenders with cauliflower mac. Cut tenders into strips for the 3-year-old. Keep the kid apple portion small.
